# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 1, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the NHS Maternity Pay Calculator (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

**Key Point: We do NOT collect, store, or process any personal information about you or your maternity pay calculations.** The Service operates entirely in your web browser (client-side), meaning your salary, FTE, and other inputs **never leave your device**.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

### 2.1 No Personal Data
We **do NOT** collect:
- Your name, email address, or contact details
- Your NHS Trust, pay band, or salary
- Your maternity pay calculations or results
- Your FTE percentage, holiday accrual, or KIT days
- Any personally identifiable information (PII)

### 2.2 How the Calculator Works
All calculations are performed **locally in your browser** using JavaScript. Your inputs (salary, FTE, leave duration) are:
- Processed entirely on your device
- **NOT sent to our servers**
- **NOT stored in any database**
- **NOT shared with third parties**

When you close the calculator, your data is **permanently deleted** from your browser (unless you save it locally using browser features like bookmarks).

## 3. Information We DO Collect (Anonymous Analytics)

### 3.1 Google Analytics
We use **Google Analytics 4** to understand how users interact with the Service. Google Analytics collects **anonymous, aggregated data** such as:
- Number of visitors and page views
- Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)
- Browser type (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.)
- Geographic location (country/region level only, e.g., "United Kingdom")
- Time spent on the Service
- Which features are used (e.g., "part-time calculator" vs. "full-time calculator")

**What Google Analytics Does NOT Collect:**
- Your name, email, or NHS Trust
- Your salary or maternity pay calculations
- Your IP address (we enable **IP anonymization** in Google Analytics)

**Data Retention:** Google Analytics data is retained for **26 months** and then automatically deleted.

**Third-Party Privacy Policy:** [Google Analytics Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 3.2 Cookies
We use **essential cookies** and **analytics cookies**:

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|-------------|---------|----------|------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics (user ID) | 2 years | Analytics |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics (session ID) | 2 years | Analytics |

**You can disable cookies** in your browser settings. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect the functionality of the calculator.

### 3.3 User Feedback (Optional)
If you choose to provide feedback via our feedback form:
- We collect **only the information you voluntarily provide** (e.g., comments, suggestions)
- If you include your email address, we may use it to respond to your feedback
- Feedback is stored securely and **not shared with third parties**
- You can request deletion of your feedback at any time

## 4. Advertising and Affiliate Links

### 4.1 Google AdSense
We display advertisements via **Google AdSense**. Google may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing history across the web (not your calculator inputs).

**Third-Party Privacy Policy:** [Google Ads Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

**Opt-Out:** You can opt out of personalized ads by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

### 4.2 Affiliate Links
The Service may include affiliate links to third-party products (e.g., insurance, savings accounts). If you click these links:
- You will be directed to the third party's website
- We may receive a commission if you make a purchase (at no extra cost to you)
- The third party's privacy policy applies once you leave our Service

**We do NOT share your calculator inputs or personal data with affiliate partners.**

## 5. How We Use Collected Data

We use anonymous analytics data to:
- Understand how users interact with the Service (e.g., which features are popular)
- Improve the calculator's accuracy and user experience
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Monitor for abusive or fraudulent usage

**We do NOT:**
- Sell, rent, or share your data with third parties (except analytics/ad providers as disclosed above)
- Use data for marketing or profiling
- Create individual user profiles or track you across websites

## 6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Even though we collect minimal data, you have the following rights:

### 6.1 Right to Access
You can request a copy of any data we hold about you (likely to be minimal or none).

### 6.2 Right to Rectification
You can request correction of inaccurate data (though we don't store personal data).

### 6.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
You can request deletion of any data we hold (e.g., feedback submissions).

### 6.4 Right to Object
You can object to analytics tracking by:
- Disabling cookies in your browser
- Using browser extensions like uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger
- Opting out of Google Analytics: [Google Analytics Opt-Out](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

### 6.5 Right to Data Portability
If we hold any data about you, you can request it in a portable format (e.g., JSON, CSV).

### 6.6 Right to Complain
If you believe we are not complying with UK GDPR, you can lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**:
- Website: [https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/)
- Phone: 0303 123 1113

## 7. Data Security

### 7.1 No Data Stored = Maximum Security
Because we **do not collect or store** your maternity pay calculations, there is **no risk of data breaches** involving your personal financial information.

### 7.2 Website Security
We use industry-standard security measures:
- **HTTPS encryption** (all data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted)
- Regular security updates and monitoring
- Third-party services (Google Analytics, AdSense) are compliant with UK GDPR

### 7.3 Client-Side Processing
Your calculations happen **entirely in your browser**. Even if our servers were compromised, attackers could **not access your salary or pay calculations** because we never receive them.

## 8. International Data Transfers

### 8.1 Google Analytics and AdSense
Google (based in the USA) processes analytics and ad data under **UK GDPR Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**, which provide equivalent privacy protections.

### 8.2 Your Calculator Data
Your maternity pay calculations **never leave your device**, so there are no international data transfers of your personal information.
